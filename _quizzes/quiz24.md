**Question 1:**  True or false.

An **eigenfunction of the Laplacian** on a domain $$\Omega\subseteq\mathbb{R}^2$$ with eigenvalue $$\lambda$$ is a nonzero function $$\psi(x,y)$$ satisfying

$$\psi_{xx} + \psi_{yy} + \lambda \psi = 0$$

and which goes to zero at the boundary $$\partial\Omega$$ of the domain.

**Question 2:**  Numerical.

Determine the smallest eigenvalue of the Laplacian on the square $$[0,\pi]\times [0,\pi]$$.
Enter your answer accurate up to three decimal places.

**Question 3:**  True or false.

Eigenvalues of the Laplacian are important, since they arise from using
separation of variables to solve the wave equation with Dirichlet boundary
conditions on a bounded domain.

**Question 4:**  True or false.

If $$s$$ is a root of the $$n$$'th Bessel function, then

$$\psi(r,\theta) = (3\cos(n\theta) + 4\sin(n\theta))J_n(sr)$$

is an eigenfunction of the Laplacian on the unit disk with eigenvalue $$s$$.
